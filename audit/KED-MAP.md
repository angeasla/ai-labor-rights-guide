# ΚΕΔ (ΠΔ 62/2025) — Topic → Article Reference Map

**Codex:** Κώδικας Εργατικού Δικαίου (ΚΕΔ), **Π.Δ. 62/2025**, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 121/11.07.2025.
**Purpose:** rewrite corpus citations to the form `ΚΕΔ (ΠΔ 62/2025) άρθρο X (originating: Ν. ....)`.
**Compiled:** 2026-07-13. **Primary source:** official ΦΕΚ Α΄121 PDF (gslegal.gov.gr), full text extracted and read article-by-article, cross-checked against the code's own **ΠΙΝΑΚΑΣ ΚΩΔΙΚΟΠΟΙΗΤΙΚΩΝ ΚΑΙ ΚΩΔΙΚΟΠΟΙΟΥΜΕΝΩΝ ΔΙΑΤΑΞΕΩΝ** (the official codifying→codified correspondence table appended to the ΦΕΚ), ELINYAE and hli.gov.gr.

> **How to read the ΚΕΔ number vs. originating law.** The ΚΕΔ renumbers everything into one continuous sequence (arts 1–588). The originating-law article is almost always different (e.g. overtime is **ΚΕΔ art. 194** but codifies **Ν. 2874/2000 art. 4**). Where a provision was later amended by the "Δίκαιη Εργασία για Όλους" law (**Ν. 5239/2025**) or by Ν. 5297/2026 / Ν. 5316/2026, the **ΚΕΔ article number is unchanged** — only content was amended. Cite the ΚΕΔ number.

## 2. ΚΕΔ overall structure (Books / Parts)

The ΚΕΔ consolidates **individual + collective labour law + occupational health & safety + labour supervision** into one code of **588 articles** in **3 Books** (Βιβλία). It repeals π.δ. 80/2022 ("Κώδικας Ατομικού Εργατικού Δικαίου").

- **ΒΙΒΛΙΟ ΠΡΩΤΟ — ΑΤΟΜΙΚΟ ΕΡΓΑΤΙΚΟ ΔΙΚΑΙΟ** (Individual Labour Law), **arts 1–367**
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Α΄ Έννοια της σύμβασης εργασίας & υποχρεώσεις των μερών (arts 1–15) — *incl. probation art. 2*
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Β΄ Ίση μεταχείριση & αξιοπρεπής εργασία (arts 16–69) — *anti-discrimination, gender equality, harassment/violence*
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Γ΄ Κατάρτιση & είδη ατομικών συμβάσεων (arts 70–140) — *transparency (Οδ. 2019/1152), fixed-term, part-time, temp-agency, **telework 130**, digital platforms, apprenticeship, disability-quota 83–93*
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Δ΄ Όροι εργασίας (arts 141–322) — *pay & minimum-wage process, **working time 169–219**, **leaves 220–255**, posting of workers, minors*
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Ε΄ Λύση & μεταβίβαση της εργασιακής σχέσης (arts 323–367) — *dismissal/severance, collective redundancies, TUPE*
- **ΒΙΒΛΙΟ ΔΕΥΤΕΡΟ — ΔΙΚΑΙΟ ΣΥΛΛΟΓΙΚΩΝ ΕΡΓΑΣΙΑΚΩΝ ΣΧΕΣΕΩΝ** (Collective Labour Relations), **arts 368–490**
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Α΄ Συνδικαλιστικές οργανώσεις (arts 368–393)
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Β΄ Συλλογικές συμβάσεις & εργασιακές διαφορές (arts 394–421) — *incl. **strike 415–421***
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Γ΄ Η έννομη τάξη της εκμετάλλευσης (arts 422–490) — *work rules, **works councils 425–441**, information/consultation, European works councils, SE/SCE involvement*
- **ΒΙΒΛΙΟ ΤΡΙΤΟ — ΔΙΚΑΙΟ ΠΡΟΣΤΑΣΙΑΣ ΤΗΣ ΥΓΕΙΑΣ & ΤΗΣ ΑΣΦΑΛΕΙΑΣ – ΕΠΟΠΤΕΙΑ ΤΗΣ ΕΡΓΑΣΙΑΣ** (OSH + Labour Supervision), **arts 491–588**
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Α΄ Προστασία της υγείας & ασφάλειας των εργαζομένων (arts 491–565) — *safety technician, occupational physician, ΕΥΑΕ*
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Β΄ Εργατικό ατύχημα (arts 566–?)
  - ΜΕΡΟΣ Γ΄ Διοικητική εποπτεία της εργασίας (arts 567–588) — *Επιθεώρηση Εργασίας, Π.Σ. ΕΡΓΑΝΗ ΙΙ, **digital work card 580**, penalties*

## 3. Domains the ΚΕΔ does NOT cover (keep citing the original laws)

The ΚΕΔ is a **labour-law** code only. It does **not** codify — and these corpus domains must stay on their own citations:

- **Social insurance / e-EFKA contributions & rules** → **Ν. 4387/2016** (the ΚΕΔ itself cross-refers to Ν. 4387/2016, e.g. art. 199 §2 for multiple insurance; art. 166 just says contributions follow insurance law).
- **Pensions (κύρια/επικουρική σύνταξη, pension formulas)** → **Ν. 4387/2016** and predecessor pension legislation.
- **Income tax on wages / payroll tax** → **Ν. 4172/2013 (ΚΦΕ)** and Ν. 4174/2013 / Ν. 5104/2024 (procedure).
- **Unemployment benefits (ΔΥΠΑ/ΟΑΕΔ eligibility & amounts)** → Ν. 1545/1985, Ν. 4921/2022 etc. *(Note: the ΚΕΔ does contain the ΔΥΠΑ wage-guarantee-on-insolvency mechanism, arts 159–168 ← Οδ. 2008/94/ΕΚ — that part IS in the ΚΕΔ.)*
- **Minimum-wage AMOUNT** → set annually by ΚΥΑ; the ΚΕΔ codifies only the **process** (art. 141 / 141Α).
- **Seafarers' labour law, public-sector/civil-servant employment (Ν. 3528/2007)** → outside the ΚΕΔ's private-sector scope.

For payroll/leave/pension/unemployment **calculators**, the deterministic formulas remain governed by Ν. 4387/2016, Ν. 4172/2013, α.ν. 539/1945 (leave days) and Ν. 2112/1920 / Ν. 3198/1955 (severance) — but for the last two, prefer the **ΚΕΔ article** (severance arts 333–341; annual leave arts 220–226) as the citeable current text.

## 4. Corrections to prior assumptions

- **Parallel employment is NOT ΚΕΔ art. 189.** Art. 189 = "Καθιέρωση χρονικών ορίων εργασίας" (the 40h full-time rule + 4-day-week reference in §2). **Parallel employment / no-exclusivity is ΚΕΔ art. 199 §2.**
- **6th-day +40% is NOT art. 182Β/182Γ** (that was the old Ν. 4808/2021 numbering). In the ΚΕΔ it is **arts 191 (non-continuous) and 192 (continuous)** at **+40%**; illegal 6th-day (breach of 5-day system) is **art. 196 at +30%**.
- **Overtime art. 194 confirmed**, and **ΔΧΕ art. 202 confirmed** — both as the task hinted, though the originating law of art. 194 is Ν. 2874/2000 art. 4 (Ν. 5239/2025 art. 7 was a later *amendment*, not the origin).
