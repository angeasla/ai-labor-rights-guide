## 1. "+50% αυτόματα" civil surcharge on unpaid δώρα — LEFT AS-IS, contested — HIGH
- **Where:** `doro-xristougennon.md` (Κυρώσεις pt.1 "συν προσαύξηση 50%" + closing "χρωστούν και το **+50% αυτόματα**") and the identical two spots in `doro-pasxa.md`.
- **Finding:** NO primary/authoritative source I fetched supports an **automatic 50% civil surcharge** for late δώρα. The confirmed consequences are: (a) the δώρο is δεδουλευμένη αποδοχή, claimable with **legal interest (τόκος υπερημερίας)** + 5-yr παραγραφή; (b) **criminal** liability ΑΝ 690/1945 (6 mo + fine, αυτόφωρο); (c) **administrative** fine by the Επιθεώρηση Εργασίας. Two sources (odigostoupoliti.eu; tovima.gr) that itemise the penalties **omit any 50% surcharge**. One WebSearch synthesis loosely mentioned a **criminal fine "25%–50%"** of the delayed amount — a *fine range*, NOT an automatic civil top-up, and unconfirmed against ΦΕΚ text.
- **Why not edited:** per the "skip → human-review if unconfirmable/contested" rule, I did not assert a basis for it, nor delete it (cannot prove the negative to the standard required). The claim is pre-existing corpus content.
- **Recommendation:** a human should either (i) **remove** the "+50% αυτόματα" wording, or (ii) replace it with the confirmed regime — "το δώρο οφείλεται ως δεδουλευμένη αποδοχή με τόκο υπερημερίας, πλέον ποινικών (ΑΝ 690/1945) και διοικητικών κυρώσεων". The militant tone survives either way. Sources: https://www.odigostoupoliti.eu/pinikes-kirosis-diadikasia-aftoforou-gia-mi-katavoli-dorou-eorton/ · https://www.tovima.gr/2026/03/13/finance/doro-pasxa-2026-mexri-pote-katavalletai-pos-ypologizetai-paradeigmata/
