- **What:** Ν.5053/2023 art.9 does **not** literally state "13 hours". - **Confidence:** High that the 11h rest applies and is inviolable; the 13h number is a necessary inference, not a verbatim statutory figure.
- **Also:** the **ΚΕΔ art.199 §2** number for parallel employment is taken from KED-MAP (conf H), not re-fetched from the ΦΕΚ Α΄121 body this session.
