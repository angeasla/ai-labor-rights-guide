## 1. `syntaxi/epikouriki-teka.md` — Επικουρική / ΤΕΚΑ (Ν.4826/2021) — SOUND

- **CONFIRMED — 3%+3% = 6% from 1.6.2022; 3,25%+3,25% = 6,5% until 31.5.2022.** e-ΕΦΚΑ circular /
  teka.gov.gr / CURRENCY-BRIEF §3. Applies to both ΤΕΚΑ and e-ΕΦΚΑ (ex-ΕΤΕΑΕΠ) auxiliary.
- **CONFIRMED — State guarantee = REAL (τιμαριθμοποιημένη) value of contributions, art.60.**
  Ν.4826/2021 **art.60 = "Κρατική εγγύηση"** (title verified, taxheaven TOC; opengov). State
  guarantees a minimum auxiliary pension computed on the real value of total paid contributions — a
  floor, NOT a positive-return or fixed-amount guarantee. Article's critical framing is accurate.
- **CONFIRMED — Return of contributions if <15 yrs, art.53.** Ν.4826/2021 **art.53 = "Επιστροφή
  εισφορών"** (title verified) — returns contributions in real value at general retirement age.
- **CONFIRMED — Capitalisation / individual accounts / defined-contribution; ΤΕΚΑ = ΝΠΔΔ;
  first-insured from 1.1.2022 → ΤΕΚΑ, pre-2022 stay PAYG via e-ΕΦΚΑ/ex-ΕΤΕΑΕΠ.** CURRENCY-BRIEF §5.
- **CONFIRMED — Optional transfer: born ≥1.1.1987 (i.e. <35), from 1.1.2023.** taxheaven ΤΕΚΑ Q&A.
  Article already hedges "επιβεβαίωσε στο teka.gov.gr… αν είναι αμετάκλητη" — good.
- **UNVERIFIABLE (minor, not refuted)** — "όλοι όσοι γεννήθηκαν από 1.1.2004" mandatory ΤΕΚΑ: not
  separately quoted from statute, but follows from the first-insured-2022 rule (2004 cohort turns 18
  in 2022). Left as-is.

## 2. `syntaxi/efapax.md` — Εφάπαξ (art.35 Ν.4387/2016) — SOUND

- **CONFIRMED — art.35 Ν.4387/2016, replaced by art.31 Ν.4670/2020; managed by Κλάδος Εφάπαξ
  Παροχών e-ΕΦΚΑ (πρ. ΕΤΕΑΕΠ).** lawspot art.35 / opengov art.31.
- **CONFIRMED — two-part calc; Τμήμα Α΄ (έως 31.12.2013) = 60% × αποδοχές αναφοράς × έτη for 4%
  contributors; αποδοχές αναφοράς = μ.ό. τελευταίας 5ετίας έως 31.12.2013; applies to αποχωρήσεις
  από 1.9.2013.** lawspot art.35 verbatim.
- **CONFIRMED (in substance) — "75% όταν η εισφορά ήταν 5%."** The statute says the 60% "**θα
  αναπροσαρμόζεται αναλογικά ως προς το ύψος της εισφοράς**" (council decision + actuary, cap 100%),
  not a hard-coded 75%. But 60%×(5/4)=75%, so the 5% figure is the correct proportional result and
  the article also states the proportional rule. Acceptable; no edit.
- **CONFIRMED — Τμήμα Β΄ (από 1.1.2014) = NDC / νοητή κεφαλαιοποίηση, notional (not market) return;
  employee εισφορά 4%** (art.35 references "μισθωτούς με εισφορά 4%").
- **CONFIRMED (KEY) — coverage NOT universal.** Article correctly limits εφάπαξ to those insured in a
  ταμείο/κλάδο/τομέα/λογαριασμό πρόνοιας and explicitly rebuts "όλοι παίρνουν εφάπαξ." No overclaim.
- **UNVERIFIABLE (minor)** — "νόμιμος χρόνος διεκπεραίωσης 6 μήνες", priority for αναπηρία ≥50%, and
  "4% από 1.1.2017" (the 4% rate itself is confirmed; the exact start date not separately sourced).
  Procedural/low-risk; not refuted, left as-is.

## 3. `syntaxi/syntaxi-thanatou.md` — Σύνταξη θανάτου (art.12 Ν.4387/2016) — FIXED

- **CONFIRMED — Επιζών σύζυγος 70%** (was 50%); **children 25% each to age 24** (continues if
  studying; lifelong if ≥67% disability); **aggregate cap = deceased's pension (100%)**. e-ΕΦΚΑ /
  lawspot art.19 Ν.4611/2019 / CURRENCY-BRIEF §5.
- **CONFIRMED — 3-year marriage/σύμφωνο rule; exceptions** (accident/homicide; child born or
  legitimated; widow pregnant). 5-yr → 3-yr change and **age-55 abolition** by Ν.4611/2019 art.19,
  ισχύς 17.5.2019. lawspot art.19.
- **REFUTED → FIXED — αναβίωση exception combined duration.** Was "≥5 έτη"; e-ΕΦΚΑ verbatim = "**≥3
  έτη**". Corrected (see Fixes #1).
- **CONFIRMED — post-3-year reduction to 35% (−50%) if working / own pension; full 70% kept if ≥67%
  disabled.** e-ΕΦΚΑ + CURRENCY-BRIEF §5. NB e-ΕΦΚΑ nuance: the 50% cut applies **pro-rata for the
  days actually worked/self-employed**; the article's blanket "μειώνεται κατά 50%" is a fair
  simplification (enrichment opportunity, not an error). "Ψαλίδι not yet abolished as of 13.7.2026"
  is accurate.
- **CONFIRMED — προσωρινή σύνταξη = 50% της οριστικής.** e-ΕΦΚΑ «Ποσό» verbatim.
- **CONFIRMED — work-accident/occupational-disease death: min €893,75 from 1.1.2026.** e-ΕΦΚΑ «Ποσό»
  verbatim + workenter. (For completeness, e-ΕΦΚΑ also sets an ordinary death-pension floor
  €418,95→€446,87 by insurance years; article need not add it.)
- **CONFIRMED — divorced-spouse conditions** (γάμος ≥10 έτη, διατροφή, μη νεότερος γάμος/σύμφωνο,
  εισόδημα ≤ όριο, διαζύγιο όχι με δική του υπαιτιότητα). e-ΕΦΚΑ verbatim — all five present and
  current (the no-fault condition IS still in force).
- **CONFIRMED (in substance) — γάμος μετά τη συνταξιοδότηση: κλιμακωτή μείωση.** e-ΕΦΚΑ confirms a
  graduated reduction by age gap (e.g. −1%/έτος for the 10–20 age-difference band). Article's
  "1–5% ανά έτος… ανάλογα με διαφορά ηλικίας και διάρκεια γάμου" is directionally correct; exact
  scale not fully pinned. Low risk; left as-is.
